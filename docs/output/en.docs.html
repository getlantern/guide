Accounts

How Lantern Pro accounts work: creating an account, signing in, managing devices, and keeping access to your subscription.

Create a Lantern Pro Account

[!NOTE] Accounts are only for Lantern Pro. The free version of Lantern does not use an account.

What you will need depends on your platform:

Platform Payment Method Plans Available When You Add an Email
iOS Apple App Store 1-month, 1-year After purchase (optional)
Android (Google Play) Google Play 1-month, 1-year After purchase (optional)
Android (sideloaded) Card (Stripe) or Alipay 1-month, 1-year, 2-year Before purchase (required)
Desktop (Windows, macOS, Linux) Card (Stripe) or Alipay 1-month, 1-year, 2-year Before purchase (required)

All card purchases are auto-renewing subscriptions. Alipay purchases are one-time payments.

Choose a Plan

  1. Select the Upgrade to Pro banner on the home screen, or open the menu and choose the upgrade option

  2. Select a plan from the Plans screen

  3. On iOS and Google Play, the store payment prompt appears now, and you add your email after paying. On desktop and sideloaded Android, you create your account first and pay after.

Add Your Email

On the Add Your Email screen you can:

  • Enter an email address - a 6-digit confirmation code is sent to your email. Enter it on the Confirm Email screen. If it does not arrive, check spam and select Resend Email. See also Not Receiving Emails from Lantern.

  • Continue with Google or Continue with Apple - your account is created instantly from your Google or Apple identity. No confirmation code or password is needed. Remember which one you used: you must sign in the same way later.

  • Continue without Email (iOS and Google Play only) - your subscription works on this device only. A valid email address is required to use your subscription on other devices. You can add an email later; see Add an Email to an Account Created Without One.

If you see a Create Account Error saying the sign-up went wrong, the email address may already have an account. Choose Sign In instead.

Pay (desktop and sideloaded Android)

On the Choose Payment Method screen, pick card or Alipay. Card payments are processed by Stripe, and Alipay opens a payment page in your browser.

If you have paid but your subscription does not show as active right away, wait a few hours and restart Lantern. If it still does not show, see I paid but my account is not showing Pro.

Create a Password (email sign-up only)

Passwords must contain at least 8 characters, including an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number, and a special character. After setting your password, your account is ready and Lantern Pro is active.

Sign In to Lantern

  1. Select Sign in from the menu on the home screen

  2. On the Sign In to Lantern screen, choose the same method you signed up with:

    • Email: enter your email, select Sign in with Email, then enter your password
    • Continue with Google or Continue with Apple

[!TIP] If you signed up with Google or Apple, you do not have a Lantern password. Use the matching Google or Apple button to sign in, or select Forgot your password? to set a password for the first time. See Setting and Resetting Lantern Pro Password.

Device Limit and Removing Devices

Lantern Pro works on up to 5 devices per account. If you sign in on a sixth device, the Device Limit Reached screen lists your linked devices. Select a device you no longer use, then select Remove Device and Sign In - sign-in continues automatically after the device is removed.

If you do not recognize a device in the list, it is usually an old phone or computer where you previously installed Lantern. Removing it does not affect your other devices.

Change Your Account Email

  1. Open the menu and go to Account

  2. Select Change Email and confirm your password

  3. Enter your new email address - a 6-digit confirmation code is sent to the new address

  4. Enter the code on the Confirm Email screen

Your new email becomes your login email once verified.

Restore Your Purchase on a New Device

  1. Install Lantern and select the Upgrade to Pro banner

  2. Select the 3-dot menu at the top right of the Plans screen

  3. Select Restore Purchase

  4. Sign in with your account email (or Google/Apple) - your Pro status is restored on this device

Manage or Renew Your Subscription

Open the menu and go to Account to see your Lantern Pro email and your expiration or renewal date.

  • Purchased on iOS: manage your subscription from your Apple ID account settings

  • Purchased on Google Play: manage your subscription from your Google Play account settings

  • Purchased on desktop or sideloaded Android: use the Manage Subscription option in Account settings

When a subscription expires, select Renew Pro Subscription to start a new purchase.

Add an Email to an Account Created Without One

If you purchased on iOS or Google Play and chose Continue without Email, your subscription is tied to that device only. To use it elsewhere:

  1. On the original device, open the menu and select Account

  2. On the Update Pro Account prompt, select Add Email

  3. Enter your email, confirm it with the 6-digit code, and create a password

You can then sign in on your other devices.

Legacy Pro Users: Set Your Password

If you had Lantern Pro before version 9, you need to set a password once in the new app.

On your existing device: open the menu and select Account. On the Set Account Password prompt, select Set Password, confirm your email with the verification code, and create a password.

On a new device: go to the sign-in screen, enter your Pro email, select Forgot your password?, and set a password through the email verification flow. Then sign in with your new password.

Log Out of Lantern

Open the menu, go to Account, and select Logout. Logging out switches Lantern to the free version on that device; your subscription is unaffected and you can sign back in at any time.

Common Account Issues

Lantern asks me for a password, but I signed up with Google or Apple

Use the Continue with Google or Continue with Apple button on the sign-in screen instead of Sign in with Email. Alternatively, select Forgot your password? to set a password for the first time.

I am not receiving the verification code

Check your spam or junk folder, then select Resend Email on the Confirm Email screen. Some email providers block our messages entirely; see Not Receiving Emails from Lantern.

I paid but my account is not showing Pro

Payments can take time to be reflected. Wait a few hours, then restart Lantern. If your account still does not show Pro, contact support with your receipt; see Report a Payment Problem.

Purchase was not completed

This appears when a store payment was cancelled, the payment method failed, or the app closed during purchase. Check that your App Store or Play Store payment method is valid, then try again from the Plans screen.

Features

Split Tunneling

Split tunneling specifies which apps and websites use proxies, and which connect directly. Because routing a VPN connection through a proxy adds some latency and speed reduction, the fastest connection will only proxy traffic that requires it. Blocked or restricted content can be routed through proxies, while the remaining traffic connects directly for the fastest speeds possible.

Try enabling split tunneling if you are noticing slower than ideal overall network speeds, and experiment with which apps or sites benefit the most!

see also: https://lantern.io/faq#privacy-4


Free Tier Data Cap

How much data does the free tier give me?

Free users get 244 MB of data per day (about 256 MB in decimal terms). The cap resets every 24 hours and is the same on Windows, macOS, Android, and iOS.

What happens when I hit the cap?

Lantern does not disconnect you. Your download speed is reduced to about 128 Kbit/s until the next daily reset. Uploads are limited to about 5 Mbit/s, which is high enough that sending requests still works normally. You will receive notifications at 50%, 80%, and 100% of your daily usage, and the home screen shows when the cap has been reached along with the reset time.

Are there places where the free tier has no cap?

Yes. Free users in Iran, Russia, and Belarus have unlimited data. We are able to offer this because grant funding supports uncensored access in those locations, and because international sanctions and payment-processor restrictions make Pro subscriptions difficult or impossible to purchase there.

How do I remove the cap everywhere else?

Lantern Pro removes the daily limit worldwide.

Verify a File Download

How to validate that a client application file is authentic, even if downloaded through an untrusted network.

Hash functions can be used to create a unique digital signature – a hash – for any file. Lantern captures this hash when our application is compiled and publishes them on GitHub: lantern-binaries. Users can run the same hash function (SHA256) on downloaded copies to assure they are an exact match, proving the file to be an authentic and unaltered copy of the original.

Most operating systems have built-in hash functions. Follow the steps below for your operating system to generate the hash for a file.

[!IMPORTANT] Make sure to replace C:\file\path\my_file.exe or /path/to/my_file with the actual path to the file.

MacOS or Linux

From your preferred terminal application, run the following command:

openssl dgst -sha256 /path/to/my_file

Windows

Command Prompt

  1. Press Windows+R to open the Run box

  2. Type cmd and click OK.

  3. The Command Prompt window will open.

  4. Run the following command:

    certutil -hashfile C:\file\path\my_file.exe SHA256

    Power Shell

    1. Press Windows+R to open the Run box
    2. Type powershell and click OK.
    3. The Windows PowerShell window will open.
    4. Run the following command: console Get-FileHash C:\file\path\my_file.exe -Algorithm SHA256

Troubleshooting

Not Receiving Emails from Lantern

This applies to any email Lantern sends: confirmation and verification codes, password resets, device linking codes, receipts, and download links.

Some email providers, especially Chinese consumer providers, block or delay messages from services hosted outside mainland China. Known affected domains include qq.com, 163.com, 126.com, yeah.net, sina.com, sina.cn, aliyun.com, sohu.com, tom.com, 21cn.com, and foxmail.com.

If an email does not arrive:

  1. Check your spam or junk folder

  2. Use the resend option on the screen you are on (for example Resend Email on the Confirm Email screen)

  3. If it still does not arrive and you use one of the providers above, change your account email to a known reliable provider:

  • Outlook

  • iCloud Mail

  • Yahoo Mail

  • Gmail

  • Proton Mail - a free, nonprofit, privacy focused email provider. In China, Proton mail require a VPN.

Reset Proxy Configuration

Windows

Some Windows applications (like WhatsApp) may have custom proxy rules that do not route through Lantern proxies. This can be solved most easily by using the web version of the application, because Lantern proxies browser traffic by default. Another option is to reset any app-specific proxy rules:

  1. Open Command Prompt or PowerShell (right click to “Run as administrator”)

  2. Run the command netsh winhttp reset proxy

  3. Restart your computer

  4. Open Lantern and connect

Log Retrieval

If you can’t submit an issue report from within the app, you can still share your logs with support.

Share logs from the app (all platforms)

The quickest way to send your logs is directly from the app. This works on desktop and mobile, including iOS and Android:

  1. Open Lantern and go to Support.

  2. Tap Diagnostic Logs.

  1. On the Diagnostic Logs screen, tap the Share icon in the top-right corner.

  1. Choose how to send the logs (for example, email them to support@lantern.io) and send.

Retrieve the log file manually (desktop)

If you’d rather attach the log file yourself, Version 9 stores logs in a new location, so use the section that matches your version.

Version 9 (current)

  • Windows: Open File Explorer and go to C:\Users\Public\Lantern\logs

  • macOS: Open Terminal, run open /Users/Shared/Lantern/Logs

  • Linux: Open Terminal, run xdg-open ~/.local/share/org.getlantern.lantern/logs

Select the newest lantern.log file and attach it to an email to support@lantern.io.

Advanced: VPN service log (Windows and Linux only)

On Windows and Linux, Lantern runs a separate background service that handles the VPN connection itself, and it keeps its own log apart from the main app. Support may ask for this log to diagnose connection or “no traffic” problems.

  • Windows: Open File Explorer, paste C:\ProgramData\Lantern into the address bar, and press Enter (this folder is hidden by default)

  • Linux: Open Terminal, run xdg-open /var/log/lantern

Attach the newest lantern.log from that folder in addition to the log above.

Legacy (before Version 9)

  • Windows: In File Explorer, enable hidden items (View menu), then go to C:\Users\<username>\AppData\Roaming\Lantern\Logs

  • macOS: Open Terminal, run open ~/Library/Logs/Lantern

  • Linux: Open Terminal, run xdg-open ~/.lantern/logs

Select the newest lantern.log file and send it to support@lantern.io.

iOS and Android

Device security settings do not permit direct access to the log file. Use Share logs from the app above to send your logs.

Advanced Diagnostics

Problems which are difficult to diagnose with logs may require more advanced tools like packet capture.
